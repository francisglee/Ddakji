## Questions
1. **Q1.** What does a realistic four-generation Korean family born ~1935 / ~1960 / ~1988 / ~2015 look like: sibling counts, ages at marriage, children per woman, who lives with whom?
2. **Q2.** Timeline and causes of the fertility collapse, and the official and popular explanations at each stage.
3. **Q3.** How household structure changed — multi-generational homes to one-person households — and what that did to care of the old and the young.
4. **Q4.** Internal migration: the emptying of the countryside, Seoul capital-region concentration, and the 'hometown' (고향) that successive cohorts do or don't have.
5. **Q5.** Marriage patterns including international marriage and the multicultural-family (다문화) policy category; what near-future population projections (2040–2060) assume.
