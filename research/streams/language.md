## Questions
1. **Q1.** Honorifics and speech levels across generations: what a grandparent, parent, and child actually use with each other today versus in 1970.
2. **Q2.** Dialect as identity — Gyeongsang, Jeolla, Jeju, and the Hamgyong of Koryo-saram and the North — and how media flattened or revived it.
3. **Q3.** Naming: generation names (돌림자), hanja decline, the rise of pure-Korean names, and how names mark cohort.
4. **Q4.** The Korean of the diaspora — Zainichi Korean, Koryo-mar, heritage-speaker Korean — and what gets lost per generation.
5. **Q5.** Loanwords, English, and internet language by cohort; what plausible near-future drift looks like.
6. **Q6.** Romanization conventions this project should fix in `world/style-guide.md`.
