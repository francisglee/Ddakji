## Questions
1. **Q1.** Which events are the memory anchors each birth cohort carries — what would a b1940s, b1960s, b1980s, b2000s character name unprompted, and what would each cohort's parents have told them?

4. **Q4.** Which real events from 2008 to the present are fixed points any near-future tail must respect, and which are still contested in memory?
5. **Q5.** What did the peninsula's division look like in ordinary family life per era — separated families, reunions, the slow fading of the North as a place people came from?
