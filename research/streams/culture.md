## Questions
1. **Q1.** Ddakji and children's games across eras: materials, rules, where they were played, and when they vanished from streets — and the game's afterlife as adult nostalgia.
2. **Q2.** 백일 and 돌: the rites, the food, the photographs, the money, and how each cohort's version differs; what the hundredth day meant when infant mortality was real.
3. **Q3.** Food by era and class — rice as scarcity, the arrival of meat, the apartment kitchen, delivery culture.
4. **Q4.** Holidays and ancestor rites (설, 추석) as the family's annual plot: travel, labor, who cooks, who quarrels.
5. **Q5.** Drinking, work dinners, and the social calendar across cohorts; hallyu seen from inside the country.
