## Questions
1. **Q1.** For each wave: why they left, what they carried, what they kept and lost by the second and third generation — language, rites, food, names.
2. **Q2.** How each diaspora relates to both Koreas today: citizenship, 재외동포 policy, return migration, and how the homeland regards returnees.
3. **Q3.** How diaspora families mark 백일 and 돌 and ancestor rites, and where the practice diverged from the peninsula.
4. **Q4.** Zainichi, Chosonjok, and Koryo-saram as populations the division cut through — Chongryon/Mindan, the Yanbian question, Soviet deportation memory.
5. **Q5.** Adoptees: the scale and the policy history, the return movements, and the citizenship and records questions still live.
6. **Q6.** What a plausible near-future diaspora looks like — return migration under demographic decline, the new skilled waves, and who counts as Korean.

## Coverage intent
Wave × lens rather than era × lens. `testimony` and `scholarship`
weighted highest; `numbers` for scale per wave (MOFA 재외동포 statistics,
host-country censuses). Every wave needs sources in the host-country
language as well as Korean — Japanese, Russian, Chinese, Spanish,
Portuguese, German — before it counts as covered.
