# research/ — sourced notes feeding the bible

Story research: South Korean culture and history, near-future
extrapolation grounding, thematic material. (Research about *craft and
process* lives in `process/reading/` instead.)

**Epistemics** (AGENTS.md rule 10, `process/roles/researcher.md`):
every claim carries `status: unverified | verified | disputed` plus
sources, and is labeled historical fact / plausible extrapolation /
invention. Findings feed canon only via `world/backlog.md` → DR.

**Interviews** (`interviews/`, `templates/interview.md`): oral history
from real people — family, friends, and others whose experience informs
the story. The consent block in each interview doc (recorded / quotable
/ anonymize) governs all downstream use and is checked mechanically at
publish. Interviews are testimony: claims stay `unverified` until
corroborated. Raw recordings beyond a few MB live outside the repo,
linked from the doc; the transcript here is the durable artifact.

Media: reference images go in `assets/`, recorded in a note's `media:`
block with source and license; scraped material defaults
`reference-only` and never publishes.
