Four gaps surfaced by checking principles against the Authors Guild's
AI Best Practices ([reading note](../reading/authors-guild-ai-best-practices.md),
DR-0014). Each needs an author decision (→ DR if adopted):

1. **Bias review** — add explicit bias/stereotype review to the
   researcher and coverage ethics (AI research on Korean culture is
   exactly where training-data bias would bite).
2. **No style mimicry** — a principle that AI is never used to imitate
   other living writers' voices or styles.
3. **Production solidarity** — stance for future narration,
   translation, and cover-art phases; preference for legally trained
   models.
4. **Human Authored certification** — pursue the Authors Guild mark;
   the repo's provenance receipts are purpose-built evidence.
