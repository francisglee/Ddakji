# 0016 — Adopt purpose tenets & rule on the Authors Guild proposals

**Description:** Two purpose tenets join the principles — the project
as practice (with the effort test) and learning as a goal — and the
four process#0010 proposals are ruled on: bias review and no-mimicry
adopted, production solidarity adopted as stance, certification kept as
a task.

## Context
Reviewing process#0010 (four gaps from the Authors Guild check,
DR-0014), the author added the deeper layer both external guides
gesture at: this project's purpose includes self-improvement — writer,
creator, voice/authenticity, project management — and learning topics
of interest for its own sake. Purpose grounds the UChicago effort test:
if the project exists to build the author, AI that offloads craft
defeats the endeavor, not just a rule.

## Alternatives considered
- **Effort test as a binding AGENTS rule** — set aside: it is a
  criterion for *deciding* on AI uses, not a per-task check; it lives
  in principles and binds through the DR process (records proposing new
  AI uses must engage it).
- **Adopting the Human Authored certification as a principle** —
  rejected: it's an action with a trigger (querying), not a commitment;
  the disclosure stance it relies on already exists. Stays a backlog
  task.
- **Deciding production-phase specifics now** — rejected: narration/
  translation/cover choices deserve their own records when real;
  today's decision is the stance they'll start from.

### Details

**0016-1.** The effort test operationalizes both purpose tenets and
the UChicago frame (reference 2): skill is built AI-free, supervised AI
layers around it, never under it. DRs proposing new AI uses must
engage the test in Alternatives.
