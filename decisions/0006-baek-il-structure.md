# 0006 — Baek-il structured as main narrative + flashback scene

## Context
Baek-il is the project's initial pitch: a short story that serves as the
vertical slice of the Ddakji universe — testing the world, voice, and
this entire production pipeline end-to-end before committing to the
epic.

## Alternatives considered
- **Single linear narrative** — set aside: loses the temporal
  counterpoint the flashback provides.
- **Full frame story** — set aside: heavier apparatus than a short
  story wants.

## Decision
Two scenes: a main narrative and a shorter flashback. Two scene docs,
two drafts (`main-narrative`, `flashback`), wired by `outline.md`.

## Consequences
Flashback *placement* (opening vs interleaved) is deliberately not
decided here — it is an open question in `works/baek-il/backlog.md` and
will get its own SDR when resolved. Baek-il should push at least one
scene through the full pipeline (draft → coverage → revision → lock →
export → publish) before Ddakji drafting begins; lessons feed Ddakji's
backlog and the process retros.
