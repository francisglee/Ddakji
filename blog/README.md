# blog/ — writing about the making

Public posts on the process of writing the book. Meta-content, not
fiction — hence root-level, not under `works/`.

- **Drafts are human-only** (`templates/blog-post.md`; same boundary as
  book prose — anything published as your prose is written by you).
  Commit as `prose:` with `DRAFTS=1`.
- **Excerpt rule** (publish checklist): posts quote the works only from
  their already-public `publish/` folders, or with explicit override at
  `/publish` — first-publication rights are not burned casually.
- **Raw material:** `decisions/` (DRs), `process/retros/`, and
  `process/reading/` are the idea mine — "why I split facts from
  views" is a post sitting in a DR. Park ideas as `backlog/` entries.
- Pipeline: draft → `editorial/` feedback → `/publish blog` →
  `publish/` (© stamped) → web.
